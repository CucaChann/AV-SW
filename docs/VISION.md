# AV-SW Product Vision

AV-SW is a design-first platform for residential AV, lighting, networking, shading, and smart-home system design.

## Core principle
The design is the source of truth. BOMs, vendor quotes, schedules, and proposals are downstream outputs of the design.

## Primary workflows
- Upload and calibrate architectural floorplans.
- Model rooms, walls, openings, millwork constraints, and elevations.
- Design lighting, Lutron controls, shades, UniFi networking, AV, audio, video, and infrastructure.
- Design and quote QTL linear lighting runs.
- Attach real manufacturer products to placed design objects.
- Track TBDs, approvals, revisions, and coordination issues.
- Validate system dependencies with a Design Check.
- Generate schedules, BOMs, quote packages, and revision comparisons.

## Product direction
The product should help a professional designer create a credible first-pass system design, explain recommendations, surface conflicts, and preserve an audit trail. It should not pretend to replace licensed electrical review, field verification, manufacturer commissioning tools, or final engineering approval.

## Technology constraint
Prefer free/open-source software and free tiers during development.
